Jobs

Jobs you're considering, private to you. Add one (or import a whole list from the job-scoring Chrome extension), see how well it fits your resume, and make a resume and cover letter tailored to it.

Before you start

Jobs are scored and tailored against your resume, using your own Anthropic key. Two things first, in this order (this box goes away once both are done):

    The job-scoring Chrome extension exports a CSV of the postings it scored. Choose that file here: new jobs are added, and a job you already have (same link) only gets its score refreshed, so your notes and "applied" marks are never touched.

    Get the extension and how to install it.

    The job-scoring Chrome extension can send the postings it captures straight here, with no file to export and import. Make a token for each computer you use it on, paste it into the extension, and any job it sends is added to your Jobs and scored against your resume in the background. A token only lets the extension add jobs to your list; it can't read anything or sign in as you. It is shown once, so copy it now, and remove it here any time to cut that computer off.
