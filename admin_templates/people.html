People

Each person's resume lives here - never a file, never in git. The default person is who the public site and the time-management app's resume generation use.

Don't want to hand-write YAML? Upload a LinkedIn profile export (from the job-application Chrome extension's "Export LinkedIn profile" button, on your own linkedin.com/in/ page) and/or any other documents you have - old resumes as PDF/DOCX, LinkedIn's own "Get a copy of your data" export, plain text notes - and Claude will draft a resume.yaml from them into the Add Person form for you to review and edit. See README.md's "Constructing your resume.yaml" section for more on what to feed it. This can take 20-60 seconds.
