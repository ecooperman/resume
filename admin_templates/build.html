Build my resume

Bring what you already have (your LinkedIn data and old resumes), tell us about the work behind them, and a first draft is written for you to check and fix. Nothing is published, and the draft is yours alone.

iWhat happens to your files

  • Your files are read on our server. We keep only their text (never the original file), saved to your account and private to you, so when something changes you can update your resume without starting over. You can remove any file below at any time, and that deletes its text.
  • Their text is sent to Anthropic (the company behind Claude) to write the draft, using your own account key, so the cost is on your account and not ours. A draft usually costs a few cents.
  • From LinkedIn's data we read only the career parts (profile, positions, education, skills, certifications, projects and your email addresses). Your connections, messages and everything else in that file are never opened.
  • The draft is shown to you first. It only becomes your resume when you press Save, and it stays private to you.

1Your Anthropic key done

2Your LinkedIn data best source

  1. Open LinkedIn's data download page (you need to be signed in to LinkedIn)
  2. Choose the first option, Download larger data archive, then press Request archive and confirm your password. (The pick-individual-files option leaves out your jobs, so it won't work.) We only read the career parts of it and never keep the rest.
  3. LinkedIn emails you a link. It can take from ten minutes up to a day. Download the ZIP and leave it zipped.
  4. Drop the ZIP here, or choose it. LinkedIn may say it is sending the archive in two parts; the first one is all you need, so you don't have to wait for the second.

Can't wait for LinkedIn's email? Skip this: old resumes in the next step, and the notes at the end, are enough to start.

3Your old resumes optional

Every version you still have helps: PDF, Word or plain text. The more you add, the more complete the draft (it keeps the richest wording of each job).

✓What we have from you saved to your account

With Keep my files on, each file's text is kept here so you never have to add it again. A newer LinkedIn ZIP replaces the older one, and a file with the same name replaces its earlier copy.

4About your work optional, very useful later

For each client or project, tell us who it was for, what they do, the industry, whether it was regulated, what you used and what came of it. These are saved to your account (private to you) and used every time a draft is written, so when your LinkedIn or resume changes you update it and these come along. They aren't printed on your resume; they let it be tailored to a job later (for example "regulated healthcare" or "Kubernetes").

5Anything else, then draft

6Check it, fix it, save it

This is a first draft written by an AI, so read it. Change anything in the box on the left; the summary on the right follows as you type. Unless you turned off keeping files, your changes here are kept as you make them, so a reload will not lose them, but it only becomes your resume when you press Save.
