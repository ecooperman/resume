Users

Add someone

Lets them into Resume: they can create an account (a passkey is quickest) and are emailed how. Only administrators can invite. Everyone's resume is completely private to them, and you can't see anyone else's. Adding someone you removed restores them. (The dashboard's People page does the same for every app at once.)

People with access

Removing someone only stops them using Resume. What they own stays private and untouched until you delete it, and restoring them puts everything back.

Removed

These people can't sign in to Resume. Deleting their data erases every resume and access code they own and can't be undone; their email stays here, removed.
